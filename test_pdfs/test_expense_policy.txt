
    EXPENSE POLICY
    
    Policy ID: EXP-2025-001
    Effective Date: 2025-01-01
    Expiry Date: 2025-12-31
    
    Travel expenses capped at Rs. 500 per day
    Hotel expenses capped at Rs. 4000 per night
